## Recurring issues to watch (Pune)

- **Monsoon:** fungal leaf spot and root rot — the biggest killer of balcony pots.
- **Summer:** spider mites and leaf scorch on tender greens.
- **Winter:** aphids on new growth of chilli and marigold.

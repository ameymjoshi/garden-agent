## Rules of thumb (Pune)

- Summer (Mar–May): halve the cadence — most pots daily.
- Monsoon (Jun–Sep): stretch it out and watch for root rot and fungus.
- Winter (Nov–Feb): pots hold moisture longer; water in the morning.

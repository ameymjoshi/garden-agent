# Season plan

Current season: **post-monsoon (October)** — a good sowing and repotting window in Pune.

## Now (Oct)

- Sow: coriander, spinach, methi, carrots, radish, and cool-season greens.
- Repot root-bound plants while the weather is mild.
- Resume a lighter watering cadence as humidity drops and days shorten.

## Upcoming

- **Nov–Feb (winter):** peak growing season for leafy greens and root vegetables; watch
  cold nights (< 12 °C) for tender plants.
- **Mar–May (summer):** protect from heat; water daily; shade tender seedlings.
- **Jun–Sep (monsoon):** drainage and fungal management; reduce watering.
